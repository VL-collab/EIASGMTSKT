法律防線：環境許可證 (EP) 的陷阱

Legal Defense: The EP Trap

《環境影響評估條例》雖具約束力，但 EP-673/2025 條款存在顯著的「延後處理」策略。許可證中多項關於噪音與視覺的實質保障措施，被歸類為「工程展開前才提交」的計劃。這在法律執行上形同「空白支票」：

While the EIA Ordinance is binding, EP-673/2025 heavily relies on "deferred processing". Many crucial noise and visual safeguards are relegated to "submit before commencement" plans. This acts as a "blank cheque":

  • 施工噪音管理計劃 (CNMP)： 僅需在動工前2個月提交。居民現階段無法預覽具體機械組合與減噪罩佈置。 CNMP: Submitted only 2 months prior. Residents cannot preview machinery or noise enclosure layouts now.
  • 視覺緩解計劃 (VMP)： 高架橋與車站美化亦延後提交。缺乏公眾參與，極易造成視覺衝擊「米已成炊」。 VMP: Visual mitigation is delayed. Lack of public participation risks irreversible visual impacts.

三、數據背後的聲學真相

3. The Acoustic Truth Behind the Data

為什麼 7.1 到 9 分貝的差距是「致命」的？許多市民可能覺得「7分貝而已差不多吧？」但在物理學與聲學工程上，這是極度可怕的差距：

Why is a 7.1 to 9 dB gap considered "lethal"? It may seem small to laymen, but in acoustic engineering, it represents a massive disparity:

聲能釋放翻倍

Acoustic Energy Multiplication

聲音分貝使用的是對數標度。物理學上，每增加 3 dB(A)，聲波能量就會「翻一倍」。

7.1 dB(A) 代表 ART 釋放的物理聲能是 SkyShuttle 的「5 倍多」！而 9 dB(A) 差距代表了近 8 倍的聲能釋放！

Decibels use a logarithmic scale. Physically, every +3 dB(A) means the acoustic energy doubles.

A 7.1 dB(A) gap means ART releases 5x more energy than SkyShuttle! A 9 dB(A) gap is nearly 8x the energy!

人耳體感噪音「翻倍」

Double Subjective Loudness

根據生理聲學研究，當分貝值增加 10 dB(A) 時，人類大腦主觀感受到的噪聲音量會「增加一倍」。

兩者相差高達 7~9 分貝，在我們單位的臥室裡聽起來，智軌 (ART) 開過去的吵雜度幾乎是雲巴的兩倍！

Psychoacoustics show that a +10 dB(A) increase is perceived by the human brain as a doubling of loudness.

With a 7-9 dB difference, ART will sound almost twice as loud as SkyShuttle from our bedrooms!

夜間安寧 55 dB 法定防線

55 dB Nighttime Defense Line

智軌 (ART) 夜間最高達 49 dB(A)，逼近 55 dB 的法定上限。未來設備稍有老化，極易衝破標準摧毀睡眠。

相反，雲巴 (SkyShuttle) 夜間預測最高僅 40 dB(A)，為居住品質保留了極為安全且寬裕的緩衝空間 (Safety Margin)。

ART reaches 49 dB(A) at night, dangerously close to the 55 dB limit. Slight equipment aging will breach this limit.

Conversely, SkyShuttle peaks at only 40 dB(A) at night, leaving a massive and secure safety margin for residents.

方案差異與打樁噪音

Piling Noise Impacts

若強行在沐泰街採用類似 Scheme A 的近距離打樁，15 米的物理距離將完全無法阻擋強烈的結構傳導噪音侵入低層單位。方案 B (利用空中花園基礎) 更能減少噪音。

If close-proximity piling is forced on Muk Tai St, the 15m distance will fail to block severe structure-borne noise. Scheme B (using Sky Garden foundation) is vastly superior.

打鼓效應 (Drumming Effect)

Drumming Effect

若採用純鋼結構橋面，車輪滾動將引發「二次輻射噪音」。長期營運後支座 (Bearing) 老化，更會放大低頻振動，對 15 米外的低層住宅造成持續性低頻干擾。

Pure steel viaducts trigger "Secondary Radiation Noise". Aging bearings amplify low-frequency vibrations, causing persistent disturbance to nearby lower floors.

雖然這些措施在環評報告中寫得井井有條，但正如我們之前討論的，這些其實都只是全港通用的「罐頭合規措施」，並沒有專門為我們距離地盤僅 15 米的極限「貼臉」環境進行客製化。

所以，在接下來的業主大會和聯署中，我們必須以此為基礎，要求將它們升級為「智能粉塵高空自動噴霧」及「超標強制 3 分鐘自動斷電停工」等特製方案，才能真正保障生活！

While these measures look highly organized, they are actually just standard "canned compliance measures" used across Hong Kong. They have NOT been customized for our extreme 15m "close-contact" residential environment.

In our joint petition, we must demand upgrades to these standard measures, such as "Smart Aerial Auto-Misting" and "Mandatory 3-Min Power Cutoffs upon Exceedance" to truly protect our daily lives!

噪音控制措施

Noise Control

  • 嚴禁敲擊式打樁：必須採用「鑽孔灌注樁」或「套管 H 樁」。
  • 靜音型機械 (QPME)：全面選用環保署認證的極低噪音工程機械。
  • 防護防音衣物：發電機需加裝「隔音罩」；打樁機包裹「隔音布」（各降 10 dB）。
  • 移動屏障：放置 2.4米高移動屏障；高空施工需懸臂式蓋頂把聲音彈回馬路中心。
  • No Percussive Piling: Bored piles or socketed H-piles only.
  • QPME: Mandatory use of certified quiet machinery.
  • Insulation: Enclosures for generators & acoustic fabric for rigs (10 dB reduction).
  • Barriers: 2.4m barriers with cantilevered covers to reflect noise.

空氣與粉塵控制

Air & Dust Control

  • 嚴密覆蓋：泥頭車必須加裝擋板並蓋好防塵布；臨時沙石需三面圍牆並定時灑水。
  • 洗車與減速：地盤出口必設洗車輪設施，嚴防泥巴帶出沐泰街。
  • 限制柴油：柴油機械限 10 台以下，必須用超低硫柴油，盡量改用電能。
  • Strict Covering: Dump trucks covered. Stockpiles require 3-sided walls & watering.
  • Wheel Washing: Mandatory washing at exits to protect Muk Tai St.
  • Diesel Limits: Max 10 machines (ultra-low sulphur); prioritize electric.

水質防禦措施

Water Quality Defense

  • 防護渠道：施工前需建好截水渠和沉澱池，防泥水流入啟德河。
  • 泥頭鋪雨衣：雨天露天建材必須用防雨帆布完全蓋好。
  • 專人回收：化學廢水與廢油由持牌收集商妥善密封運走。
  • Catch-pits: Required to prevent muddy runoff into Kai Tak River.
  • Tarpaulins: Exposed materials must be fully covered during rain.
  • Chemicals: Waste oil collected by licensed contractors.

垃圾與廢物管理

Waste Management

  • 即日清：生活垃圾即日清理，防臭味及蚊蟲滋生。
  • 運載記錄：實施「運載記錄制度」(Trip Ticket) 追蹤每車廢料，泥土盡量現場回填。
  • 室內攪拌：水泥攪拌必須在封閉室內進行，鎖死粉塵與噪音。
  • Daily Clearance: Refuse cleared daily to prevent odor/pests.
  • Trip Ticket: Every truck load tracked; soil reused on-site.
  • Indoor Mixing: Cement mixing inside enclosed structures.
