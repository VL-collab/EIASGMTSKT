Quietness or Views?

根據 2024 年最新環評報告，3.5 公里高架運輸系統將於 2027 動工。但兩大候選系統（雲巴與智軌）各帶有致命缺點。本站為您揭示環評背後的真相。

Based on the 2024 EIA report, the 3.5km smart and green mass transit system starts construction in 2027. Both contenders have fatal flaws. We reveal the hidden truth.

系統對決：各自的致命傷

System Showdown: Fatal Flaws

SkyShuttle (雲巴)

SkyShuttle

專用導軌行駛系統 Dedicated Guide Rail
  • ✅ 極安靜：夜間預測僅35-40分貝，遠低於背景噪音，保障睡眠。 Extremely Quiet: 35-40dB at night, well below background noise.

  • ⚠️ 致命缺點：視覺壓迫與採光阻擋

    ⚠️ Fatal Flaw: Visual Blockage

    永久的灰色混凝土導軌與車站結構，對 The Henley 和 Henley Park (低層和Mansion) 低層造成嚴重視覺壓迫。

    Permanent concrete structures block natural light and views for low-level units at The Henley and Henley Park (Lower-floor and Mansion).

ART (智軌)

ART (Autonomous Rail)

虛擬軌道膠輪系統 Virtual Track Rubber Tire
  • ✅ 結構輕盈：無笨重導軌，對鄰近住宅低層景觀影響有可能較小。 Lighter Structure: No heavy rails, maybe preserving more views.

  • ⚠️ 致命缺點：低頻噪音隆隆聲

    ⚠️ Fatal Flaw: Low-Freq Rumble

    膠輪摩擦噪音源強度達 71.6 dB（近兩倍聲能）。低頻穿透力極強，雖合法規但構成實質睡眠滋擾。

    Source noise is 71.6 dB (double acoustic energy). Penetrating low-frequency rumble causes sleep nuisance despite legal compliance.

📊 營運噪音數據深度剖析 (夜間)

📊 Operational Noise Deep Dive (Night)

雖然兩種系統均 100% 符合法定夜間噪音標準 (55 dB)，但 SkyShuttle 比 ART 安靜約 7 至 9 dB(A)。在聲學上，7.1 分貝差距代表了近乎「兩倍」的聲能。距離住宅極限 15 米的情況下，感受尤為明顯。

Though both comply with the 55dB night limit, SkyShuttle is 7-9 dB(A) quieter than ART. A 7.1dB gap means nearly double the acoustic energy, highly noticeable at a 15m extreme proximity.

根據《Kai Tak Green Transit Environmental Impact Assessment Report》，政府及承建商將於施工及營運期間實施以下環境保護措施。然而，這些「標準措施」是否足以應付近在咫尺的民居，仍是一大疑問。

According to the EIA Report, the government will implement the following environmental protection measures during construction and operation. However, the adequacy of these "standard measures" for nearby residents remains questionable.

🚧 Construction Phase (2027-2031)

🏗️

噪音控制

Noise Control

  • 使用優質機動設備 (QPME) 及較寧靜施工法（如鑽孔樁代替撞擊式打樁）。
  • Use of Quality Powered Mechanical Equipment (QPME) and quieter methods (e.g., bored piling).
  • 設置臨時可移動隔音屏障 (預計減音 5-10 dB)。
  • Use of Movable Noise Barriers (expected 5-10 dB reduction).
  • 為打樁機等設備覆蓋隔音布 (預計減音 10 dB)。
  • Installation of Noise Insulating Fabric (expected 10 dB reduction).
業主質疑： 移動屏障只能阻擋低層噪音，對中高層單位毫無防護力！
Critique: Movable barriers only protect low floors, leaving mid/high floors completely exposed!
💨

塵埃與空氣質素

Dust & Air Quality

  • 嚴格執行《空氣污染管制（建造工程塵埃）規例》。
  • Strict compliance with Air Pollution Control Regulation.
  • 工地需定期灑水除塵。
  • Regular water spraying on site for dust suppression.
  • 露天物料堆放區必須使用帆布完全覆蓋。
  • Open stockpiles must be entirely covered by tarpaulin.
💧

水質與廢物管理

Water & Waste Management

  • 設置隔沙/隔泥池及隔油池，淨化地盤徑流後才排入公共排水系統。
  • Provision of sand/silt and oil/grease traps before discharging runoff into drainage.
  • 聘用持牌化學廢物收集商處理污染水。
  • Employ licensed chemical waste collectors.
  • 實施廢物管理計劃 (WMP) 鼓勵現場分類。
  • Implement Waste Management Plan (WMP) for on-site sorting.

系統與固定噪音源控制

System & Fixed Noise Control

  • 🔹 車站廣播系統 (PA System)： 使用定向揚聲器，並嚴格設定最高音量上限，避免聲音直接射向現有及規劃中的住宅區。 PA System: Use of directional loudspeakers and volume limits to avoid pointing directly at residential areas.
  • 🔹 車廠設計： 車廠將採用全封閉式建築結構，配備連續的混凝土裙樓頂蓋及隔音百葉窗，將維修噪音鎖於室內。 Depot Design: The depot will be an enclosed building with a concrete podium cover and acoustic louvers to contain maintenance noise.

景觀及視覺影響緩解

Landscape & Visual Mitigation

  • 🔹 美學設計： 車站及高架橋結構將採用現代化、輕盈及融入周邊環境的美學設計 (OM1)。 Aesthetic Design: Stations and viaducts will adopt lightweight, modern designs to blend with the surroundings (OM1).
  • 🔹 綠化補償： 實施景觀處理 (OM2) 及補償性樹木種植 (OM3)，以彌補施工期間移除的植被，降低視覺壓迫感。 Greening: Implementation of landscape treatments (OM2) and compensatory tree planting (OM3) to soften visual impacts.

⚠️ EIA's "Selective Blindness" & Construction Crises

🙈

1. 刻意排除「累積環境衝擊」

1. Exclusion of Cumulative Impacts

環評報告 2.7.3 條白紙黑字寫明，將以下近在咫尺的大型項目從累積噪音與粉塵評估中完全排除（理由是暫無施工時間表）：

EIA Sec 2.7.3 explicitly excludes the following nearby mega-projects from cumulative noise/dust assessments (claiming 'no schedules'):

  • 啟德旅遊節點 (Tourism Node)
  • Kai Tak Tourism Node
  • 啟德都會公園 (Metro Park)
  • Kai Tak Metro Park
  • 官塘避風塘行人及單車天橋
  • Kwun Tong Typhoon Shelter Footbridge

這意味著在 2027-2031 年間，居民極可能面臨多個地盤同時動工的「疊加式風暴」，而環評並未作最壞情況模擬！

This means residents may face overlapping construction dust/noise storms between 2027-2031, unsimulated in this EIA!

Fatal Blindspot: No mention of Emergency Vehicular Access (EVA). Fire engines may be trapped or unable to enter the narrowed street!
